Thesis: customer experience is the highest-leverage growth driver
Investment thesis structure
Superior Customer Experience (CX) Unlocks Sustainable, High-Margin Growth
CX Increases Retention
• ~15-20% uplift in customer lifetime value (CLV)
• -10% churn rate reduction annually
• 3x faster growth from loyalists
CX Drives Advocacy & Referrals
• 2.5x higher referral rate from promoters
• 80% of new leads from organic word-of-mouth
• Lower cost of acquisition (CAC) due to referrals
CX Improves Pricing Power
• Ability to command +10% premium vs. competitors
• 2x greater price elasticity for top-tier experiences
• Increased cross-sell/upsell success
